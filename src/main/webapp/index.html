About DreamHome Real Estate Management System

DreamHome is an enterprise-grade real estate platform designed for property buyers, sellers, renters, and agents. Developed as an academic and professional project, it combines Java backend architecture, Supabase cloud persistence, interactive Three.js 3D architectural rendering, and Google Maps geo-spatial location services.

Core Capabilities

🗺️ Google Maps Location Services: Exact coordinates, street landmarks, and one-click driving navigation for every property.
🎮 3D Architectural Studio: Interactive 360° Three.js models with day, sunset, and night lighting controls, and interior inspection.
🏛️ Multi-State Property Portfolio: Curated listings covering Karnataka, Maharashtra, Goa, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, and Delhi NCR.
🔒 Supabase REST & Cloud Database: Live synchronized PostgREST queries with in-memory caching resilience.
👤 Verified Agent & Owner Channels: Direct phone calling and WhatsApp messaging for instant site visits.
📑 Title & Clear Encumbrance Checks: RERA, MUDA, BDA, and CMDA compliance information on every listing.
